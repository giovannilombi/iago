# Recording a call

IAGO records **two separate tracks**: the Mac's audio (the voices of the others in Teams, Zoom, Meet…) and your microphone. This way it always knows which one is you, and it transcribes better.

## Start and stop
- **Click the IAGO icon** {{icona}} in the menu bar: the menu opens, with **Start recording**. While recording, the same menu has **Pause**, **Stop**, **Discard** and **Mark moment**, and the icon shows the time.
- **Right-click** (or Control-click) the icon: it starts, pauses and resumes without opening the menu. A normal click never pauses a call by mistake.
- The recording appears in **Activity** with its duration, microphone and status.
- **Meeting in person:** it starts the same way, also without any open call. If the Mac's audio stays silent, IAGO understands you were in the same room and separates the microphone's voices by itself.
- **Several people in the room during a call:** if you notice other people are with you, choose **Several people here in the room** from the menu while recording. The call will be transcribed with their voices apart.

## Mark moment
During the call, choose **Mark moment** from the menu: IAGO places a bookmark a few seconds before now, with an optional note. In the item card you find it under *Bookmarks*, and a click takes you to that point in the audio. You can create a key point directly from a bookmark.

## Mark moment with screenshot
When someone shares a slide, a table or a chart, choose **Mark moment with screenshot** (or press **⌃⌥S**): IAGO captures the screen the pointer is on, without its own windows, and adds it to the call **at that minute**.
- The Mac reads the text in the screenshot: you find it in the transcript at its minute, in search and in summaries, which can cite a slide.
- In the item card, screenshots are in the **Screenshots** section: a click opens the image with the text that was read, and from there you can listen to the call from that point.
- It needs the **Screen Recording** permission (Settings › Permissions). IAGO captures the screen only when you ask, never on its own. Every now and then macOS asks you to confirm the permission.
- In Settings › General you can choose to capture all screens.

## Shortcuts in all apps
They work even while you are in Teams or another app:

| Action | Shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| Mark moment | **⌃⌥M** |
| Mark moment with screenshot | **⌃⌥S** |
| Start / Stop recording | **⌃⌥R** |

Change or turn them off in **Settings › General › Shortcuts in all apps**: every combination includes ⌃ or ⌥, so it never takes the place of other apps' ⌘ shortcuts. If another app already uses a combination, IAGO tells you: pick another one.

## Microphone
- **Automatic** (recommended): IAGO uses the call app's microphone and follows it if you plug in or unplug your headphones.
- **Fixed**: pick one in Settings › Audio or from the menu of the IAGO icon {{icona}}.

## Without headphones: echo
Without headphones the microphone also hears the speakers, and the others' voices partly come back on your track too. **Use headphones when you can.** When transcribing, IAGO still recognizes and drops the sentences of the system track that reappear on the microphone.

## A recording is never lost
IAGO writes to disk continuously. If the Mac shuts down or IAGO quits suddenly, it recovers the interrupted recording at the next launch.

## Discard
**Discard** throws away the recording in progress; it can still be recovered from the Trash for one hour.
