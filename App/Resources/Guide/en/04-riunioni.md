# Meetings and calendar

## The suggestion to record
When a call app (Teams, Zoom, Webex, FaceTime, Slack) uses the microphone for a few seconds, or when a Calendar meeting with a call link begins, IAGO shows a **panel** at the top right with **Record** and **Ignore**.

![The suggestion to record](img/proposta.png)

- The panel stays until you answer (5 minutes at most), or 30 seconds if you prefer (Settings › Audio › Meetings).
- With overlapping meetings, one panel appears per meeting.
- In Settings you can choose: **Ask**, **Record automatically** or **Do nothing**.
- At the end of the call IAGO suggests **Stop**; it never stops on its own.

## The meeting title
IAGO takes the title from the **Mac's Calendar**. If more than one meeting is in progress and it is not sure which one it is, the panel asks you to choose. You can still edit the title in the item card.

### Connecting your work calendar
1. **System Settings › Internet Accounts** › add your work account (**Microsoft Exchange**) and, if you use it, **Google**.
2. Turn on **Calendars** for each one.
3. In IAGO: **Settings › Audio › Meetings › Use the Mac's Calendar** and allow access.

## Teams mute
Teams keeps the microphone open even when muted. With the **Accessibility** permission, IAGO reads the microphone button in the Teams window: when you are muted it records **silence** on your track, so what you say with the microphone off does not end up in the transcript.
- Turn it on in Settings › Audio › Meetings › *Follow Teams mute with Accessibility*.
- If Teams changes the button's wording, update it in *Button labels (advanced)*.
- Where IT allows it, there is also the **Teams local API** (Teams › Settings › Privacy › Third-party app API).
