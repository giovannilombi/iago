# Privacy, Claude and sharing

**The principle: nothing leaves your Mac unless you want it to.** You decide how far your content goes.

## On the Mac, by default
- Recordings, transcripts, summaries and search stay on the Mac, in an **encrypted archive** with a key in the Keychain.
- The **local engine** (summaries, suggestions, Ask) runs on the Mac: the texts go nowhere.
- No telemetry, statistics or remote crash reporters.
- The only connections IAGO makes on its own are the model downloads you ask for (from fixed addresses, with verification) and the update check. The **access log** (Settings › Privacy) lists them all.
- What you share with *Share…* leaves the Mac only the way you choose (Mail, Messages, AirDrop, a folder), and is not encrypted.

## If you want more: Claude
You can connect **Claude** to the projects you choose (chapter *Claude*):
- it reads only what you enable;
- it writes only with your confirmation;
- every read and every write goes into the access log.

## For teamwork: trusted spaces (coming soon)
To share a project with your colleagues you will put it in a **space controlled by you or your company**: a OneDrive, SharePoint, Google Drive or Dropbox folder. IAGO has no server of its own. Whoever has access to the folder has access to the project, and an icon next to the project reminds you that it is shared.

## Consent
Always tell the participants that you are recording.

## Backup
- **Settings › Backup and transfer › Export encrypted backup** creates a password-protected file with the whole archive.
- On another Mac, even during the initial setup, **Restore from backup** brings it back as it was.
- You can export the decrypted audio, with the transcripts alongside.

## Obsidian
Settings › Obsidian keeps a copy of the archive as Markdown notes in a vault, updated on its own.
