# Common problems

**"No system audio" in the test.** System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording: turn on IAGO and restart it.

**The others' voices are on my track too.** Without headphones the microphone hears the speakers: use headphones. When transcribing, IAGO still drops the sentences repeated from the system track.

**The suggestion to record does not appear.** Settings › Audio › Meetings: check "When a call starts" and the status line; try *Show a sample suggestion*.

**The meeting title is wrong or missing.** Connect your work calendar (chapter *Meetings and calendar*); with more than one meeting in progress, IAGO asks you which one.

**Teams mute is not followed.** Turn on Accessibility for IAGO; if the status line says "button not found", update the button labels.

**The summary does not start.** You need the local engine, at least Qwen 3.5 9B, and a category with a summary template.

**Names spelled wrong in the transcript.** Add them to the glossary (Settings › Transcription) and transcribe again.

**IAGO is slow.** Check in Activity what it is doing; heavy jobs run one at a time and pause during recordings.
