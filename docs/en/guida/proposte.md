# Engine suggestions

After each transcription or import, with the local engine IAGO suggests:
- decisions and tasks, each with the sentence it comes from; for a task also who does it, by when and, if someone else has to send it ("Anna will send us the files by Friday"), who you are waiting for;
- open questions (questions left without an answer), risks with their severity and client requests;
- entries that a new decision could supersede, or an open question it answers, or a request it accepts;
- names for generic speakers;
- tags, with the sentence they come from, only if the item really talks about that topic;
- project (for what is unfiled) and links to other items.

Suggestions appear in the item card and in *Key points › To confirm*: **Confirm**, **Edit…** or **Dismiss**. Nothing is applied without your confirmation; a dismissed suggestion does not come back. Automatic suggestions are turned on and off in Settings › Engine; on Macs with less than 32 GB of memory they are off by default, and you ask for them by hand with *Find suggestions*.

Every suggestion says where it comes from: "Suggested by Qwen 3.5 4B on the Mac", "Recommended by Claude" or, for a speaker's name, "Recognized by voice" (if you turned on Settings › Voices). Next to the smaller models, **small model** appears: with a larger model the suggestions are more complete. When you confirm, the key point remembers who suggested it.
